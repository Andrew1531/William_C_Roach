Featured book

Faith in the Crossfire

Faith in the Crossfire explains this struggle with clarity and depth. William C. Roach traces the philosophical and theological development of dialectical thought, examines how it operates within contemporary culture and the church, and exposes its consequences for Christian doctrine and practice. He then presents a constructive defense of objective truth, sound reason, divine revelation, biblical authority, and the historic Christian faith. Readers will come away better equipped to recognize the ideas reshaping Christianity, understand where those ideas originated, and defend the truth revealed in Jesus Christ, the eternal Logos.

Scholarship & service

A life of
serious inquiry.

Faith is not a retreat from questions. It invites us to ask better ones.

William (Bill) C. Roach, Ph.D. serves as Dean of the Norman L. Geisler School of Apologetics at Veritas International University and as Chair of Systematic Theology at Redeemer Seminary.

A voice in philosophical theology and classical apologetics, Dr. Roach is an author, editor, and lecturer whose work engages the intellectual and cultural questions facing Christianity.

His scholarship has been featured in Christianity Today and WORLD Magazine. He has also served as Past President of the International Society of Christian Apologetics.

Through books, lectures, and teaching, he continues to defend the historic Christian faith with clarity and conviction.
